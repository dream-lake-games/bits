## What am I searching for that no one else is?

Uhhhh I'm not really sure how this part comes in.

Handwave handwave handwave maybe I would say this: Games are interesting because — generally speaking — it is possible to fully understand their rules. This is hopelessly impossible for life. Games appeal to the child in us because they are approachable and friendly. But they continue to be interesting as we grow because they hide unimaginable complexity. There are (roughly) 10^18 more ways to shuffle a deck of cards than there are atoms on earth. This is humbling. Even when we strive to create something "manageable", something "pure" and "fun" we uncover patterns beyond comprehension.

Games are core to what go into a language model. Training is essentially one giant game. Every time we start a new conversation, the model starts fresh. It has the same set of rules, the same fixed outputs.

## The game?

The game is basically a rip off of [Wits and Wagers](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/20100/wits-and-wagers). Basically:

- Numeric question
- Every player submits a guess, guesses are revealed
- People place bets on which answer they think is closest without going over
- Points points points, yada yada yada.

The twist is that in my version you can make the players to agents with search enabled.
